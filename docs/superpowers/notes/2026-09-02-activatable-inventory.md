# Activatable-service inventory — schema-dbus SP1 deferral safety net (T12)

Generated by `scripts/sdbus-activatable-audit.sh` (read-only) on blakbox, 2026-09-03.
Source: `/usr/share/dbus-1/system-services/*.service` (67 services).

## Why this matters

The SP1 broker has **no bus activation**: `org.freedesktop.DBus.StartServiceByName`
is a stub, and schema-init has no systemd to hand activation off to. So a D-Bus
service that used to start on demand only stays available after cutover if the
**schema-init rail already launches its daemon eagerly** (the daemon owns its
well-known name before anyone calls it).

Two distinct failure classes hide in "not on the rail":

- **Real `Exec=`** entries are direct-activated by *stock* dbus-daemon **today**
  (no systemd needed). These **regress** the moment we cut over to schema-dbus.
- **`Exec=/bin/false` + `SystemdService=`** entries depend on *systemd*
  activation, which is **already dead** under schema-init (systemd isn't PID 1).
  These are already unavailable pre-cutover — schema-dbus does not regress them.

## Recommendation for the cutover (T13)

Rail-pin the desktop-critical daemons before the hardware flip, mirroring
`polkitd.svc` (exec=`schema-subreaper`, args=`<daemon> <flags>`, dep=`dbus`,
needs_root=1, critical=0):

- **wpa_supplicant** (wifi hosts), **accounts-daemon**, **upowerd**, **udisksd**
  — recommended pins.
- **rtkit-daemon** — optional pin.
- **ModemManager** and the 41 on-demand helpers — leave dark until the broker
  gains minimal activation in v1.1.

Pins are **not created in T12** (this task is read-only inventory). They are a
cutover artifact — create + commit them in T13 alongside `dbus.svc.sp1`, per
Jonathan's decision on which to pin.

## Deeper implication

The clean long-term fix is **minimal bus activation in the broker (v1.1)** — a
`StartServiceByName` that execs the activatable `Exec=` (there is no systemd to
defer to, so the broker itself is the activator, as classic dbus-daemon is).
That retires the whole rail-pin workaround and the "dark until v1.1" set.
